# Monthly Review — YYYY-MM

~60 minutes. Synthesis. Led by Skeptic + Therapist + a rotating lens.

## Patterns this month
What recurred — across the weekly reviews, the journal, the evidence files. The
themes you couldn't see at weekly scale.

## Candidate principles
Principles the month's evidence suggests. For each: the evidence behind it, a
confidence rating, and a falsification criterion. The Skeptic pressures each one;
borrowed-sounding rules get sent back. **Promote none without your sign-off.**

## Full-stack coherence
A deeper pass than weekly: are goals still serving the vision? Is any area
chronically starved? Is capacity realistic across all active projects?

## Status recommendations
Artifacts the agent recommends moving (`draft → active`, `active → contested`,
etc.), each with evidence. You confirm or decline.

## The deeper question (Therapist)
The pattern under the patterns — what a recurring friction is protecting, if one
surfaced. Sit with it; don't force a resolution.

## Carry forward
What this month's reflection changes about next month's focus.
