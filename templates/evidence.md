# [Short title for the instance]

Evidence files collect the raw instances that reflection draws on, sorted by the
six types below. They are the bridge between daily journal noise and committed
principles: when a pattern accumulates across several evidence entries, it becomes
a candidate principle.

## The six types

- **proud** — something you did that you'd do again, that reveals a value lived.
- **regret** — something you did (or didn't) that you wish you'd handled differently.
- **conflict** — a clash where your values pulled in different directions.
- **admired** — something *someone else* did that you respect (reveals your values
  by what you notice).
- **disliked** — something someone else did that bothered you (same, by contrast).
- **surprise** — something that violated your model of yourself or the world.

## What happened

Concrete and dated. Specific enough to be evidence, not a summary.

## What it reveals

The value, principle, or pattern this instance points to — stated as a hypothesis,
not a conclusion. (The agent may suggest; you decide.)
