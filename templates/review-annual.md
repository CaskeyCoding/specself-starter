# Annual Review — YYYY

Half a day. The blank-slate rewrite. Led by Future-Self + Biographer + Stoic.

## Blank-slate rewrite (do this FIRST)
Before looking at last year's corpus, re-derive your principles from scratch.
What do you actually live by, today, written fresh? This is the heart of the
annual — the corpus you can rebuild from memory is the corpus that's truly yours.

## The diff
Now compare the fresh rewrite against last year's principles. What survived
unchanged? What you couldn't re-derive gets retired (it was borrowed or outgrown).
What's new? **The diff is the year's real report.**

## The arc (Biographer)
The story of the year as a chapter. The through-line, the turning point, the title
you'd give it.

## The long view (Future-Self + Stoic)
Does the person you became this year match the person the older you will be glad
you became? What small compromise, compounded, needs attention? What's worth
starting next year?

## Retirements & supersessions
Principles and goals retired this year, each with what replaced it (if anything).
Nothing is deleted — the trail is the point.

## Next year's vision delta
What this rewrite changes about `vision.md`. Update it (or note that it held).
