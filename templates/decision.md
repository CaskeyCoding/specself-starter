# ADR-NNN — [Decision title]

A life-ADR captures a real fork in your life the way an architecture decision
record captures one in a system: what you chose, what you rejected, and why — so
your future self can see the reasoning, not just the result.

## Context

The situation that forced a decision. What changed, what pressure was on, what was
at stake.

## Options considered

Lay out the real alternatives honestly, including the one you rejected.

### Option A — [name]
- Pros / cons. Why kept or rejected.

### Option B — [name]
- Pros / cons. Why kept or rejected.

## Decision

What you chose, stated plainly.

## Consequences

What this commits you to, what it closes off, and what you'll watch to know if it
was right. If this decision revises a principle, name the principle id and write
the supersession on both sides.
