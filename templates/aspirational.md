# Aspirational — Who I Want to Become

The singleton `your-life/aspirational.md`. Rules you *want* to live by but
cannot yet back with evidence collect here, kept deliberately apart from the
evidence-backed `revealed` principles in `principles/<domain>/`. The gap
between this file and that folder is the most useful signal the system
produces — protect it. Never move an entry out of here without real, dated
evidence.

Each entry is principle-shaped (same discipline as `templates/principle.md`:
an actionable rule, a why, a falsifiable promotion bar), one H2 section per
entry. When evidence accumulates, the entry **graduates**: it becomes its own
file under `principles/<domain>/<slug>.md` with `kind: revealed`, and the
entry here is replaced by a one-line pointer with the graduation date.

## [the-rule-as-a-slug]

- domain: core              # core | work | relationships | health | money | integrity | learning
- added: YYYY-MM-DD

**The rule.** One actionable sentence you could follow and fail — not a virtue
word.

**Why it appeals.** What wanting this says about you. Borrowed ambitions get
caught here: if the honest answer is "someone I envy lives this way," write
that down.

**Promotion bar.** The concrete, dated evidence that would justify graduating
this to `revealed`. If you can't name it, this is a mood, not an aspiration.
