# Quarterly Review — YYYY-Qn

~90 minutes. The falsification sweep and the primary pruning tier. Led by Skeptic +
Forensic-Auditor.

## The audit (Forensic-Auditor)
What the passive trail says about where your time, money, and attention actually
went this quarter — against what you said mattered.

## Falsification sweep
Walk every `active` principle and goal. For each: is there new supporting evidence
this quarter? Any with none is auto-recommended `contested` (you confirm). Any
whose falsification criterion was met is recommended for retirement.

| Artifact | New evidence this Q? | Recommended status | Your call |
|---|---|---|---|
|  |  |  |  |

## Goals checkpoint
Each goal against its success and failure criteria. On track, drifting, met, or to
be re-scoped?

## Pruning
What comes off the list. Dead projects, fictional habits, starved areas you're
choosing to let go. Breadth without pruning is rot — this is where you cut.

## Status transitions made
The transitions you confirmed this session (the record of what changed and why).
