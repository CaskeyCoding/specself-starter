# [Habit — the recurring commitment]

State the behavior and its cadence concretely: "Train 3×/week" — not "exercise
more." A habit you can't count is one you can't keep honestly.

## Why it serves [area/principle]

The link up. A habit exists to uphold an area's standard or live out a principle.
If it serves neither, question whether it's worth tracking.

## What counts

The precise definition of a kept rep, so you can't fudge it. ("A 20-minute walk
does not count as a training session.")

## Lapse signal

When does this move to `lapsed`? (e.g., "two consecutive weeks under cadence.")
This feeds the pruning loop — a perpetually-lapsed habit is either re-scoped or
retired, not left to rot as a fiction.

## Evidence

Dated record of kept (and missed) reps. Honesty here is the whole point — the
misses are as informative as the hits.
