# Vision — Who I'm Becoming

A one-page, present-tense portrait of the person you are three-to-five years out
*if the principles hold and the work compounds*. Not a resume, not a goal list —
a portrait. Write it as if it's already true.

## The portrait

Present tense. Concrete. "I run a business that…", "My mornings are…", "My
relationships look like…". Specific enough that you'd recognize whether you got
there.

## What has to be true

The few load-bearing conditions this vision depends on. These are where your goals
(H3) come from.

## What I'm willing to trade

A vision implies costs. Name what you're willing to give up to become this person —
and what you're not. The "not" list protects your principles.

## What this is NOT

Guard against the borrowed vision — the one assembled from status, other people's
expectations, or a highlight reel. Name what you're explicitly *not* aiming for.
