# domains/ — Per-Domain Synthesis

Periodic, free-form synthesis of one domain (`core`, `work`, `relationships`,
`health`, `money`, `integrity`, `learning`) at a time, produced during
monthly+ sessions once there's enough accumulated evidence to step back and
look at the whole domain, rather than at one instance.

## No template, on purpose

Unlike everything else in `your-life/`, this folder has no matching
`templates/` file. Domain synthesis is prose: what's actually going on in
this domain right now, read across the month's journal entries and evidence,
not a form to fill in.

## Filename

Suggested: `<domain>-YYYY-MM.md` (e.g. `health-2026-07.md`). Not
basename-as-identifier the way other artifacts are (see
[`../../../templates/README.md`](../../../templates/README.md)), since a
domain can accumulate more than one synthesis over time.
