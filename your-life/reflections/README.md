# reflections/ — Reflection Material

Two children, two different jobs.

## `domains/`

Periodic per-domain synthesis: free-form notes written during monthly+
sessions that step back and look at a whole domain (work, health, money, …)
at once, rather than at one dated instance. No template, on purpose, this is
prose, not a form.

## `evidence/`

The raw material reflection draws on: single dated instances sorted into six
types (proud, regret, conflict, admired, disliked, surprise), hydrated from
[`../../templates/evidence.md`](../../templates/evidence.md). When a pattern
accumulates across several evidence entries, it becomes a candidate
principle.

See [`../../steering/methodology.md`](../../steering/methodology.md) for how
evidence and domain synthesis feed the horizons stack above them.
