# evidence/ — Raw Material for Reflection

Single dated instances, hydrated from
[`../../../templates/evidence.md`](../../../templates/evidence.md). This is
the bridge between daily journal noise and committed principles: when a
pattern accumulates across several evidence entries, it becomes a candidate
principle.

## The six types

`proud | regret | conflict | admired | disliked | surprise` (see the
template for what each means). `type:` frontmatter carries which one a given
entry is.

## Filename

`<YYYY-MM-DD>-<slug>.md`.

## Immutable once written

Evidence carries no `status:` field (see
[`../../../templates/README.md`](../../../templates/README.md)): it's a
record of what happened, not something that moves through a lifecycle. If
your read of it changes, write a new entry or say so in a later reflection,
don't edit the original.
