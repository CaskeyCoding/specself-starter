# Philosophy — The Contract

**Read every session.** This is why the system works.

## You decide, the agent organizes

> **You decide and commit. The agent organizes, connects, surfaces, and
> challenges — but never silently authors your life.**

A reflection that someone (or something) else did for you is worthless — it isn't
yours, so you won't live by it. The irreducible act is *you*, present and
engaged, making the call. The agent's job is to make that act easier and more
honest: it gathers the context, asks the sharp question, holds up the
contradiction, and remembers what you decided. It never makes the decision.

This is the line between a tool that helps you think and a tool that thinks
*instead* of you. The second kind feels productive and leaves you hollow. We are
building the first kind.

### What the agent may do without you

- Ingest and summarize your own files and any signal you paste in.
- Hydrate a session: pull the right prompts, surface what's overdue.
- Compute gaps — coverage, coherence, staleness — and report them.
- Draft *candidates* clearly labeled as candidates for you to edit or reject.

### What the agent must never do without you

- Author or edit a principle, value, vision, goal, or area.
- Decide a goal is met, a project is done, or a habit is kept.
- Mark anything `contested`, `retired`, or `met`.
- Resolve a contradiction. Surfacing it is the help; resolving it is yours.

## Spec-driven living

The rest of software engineering learned that systems get better when decisions
are written down, dated, diffable, and revisited. Lives deserve the same rigor:

- **Diffable** — you can see how your principles changed year over year.
- **Dated** — every artifact records when it was written and last reviewed.
- **Falsifiable** — a principle states what evidence would force its retirement.
  A value you can't falsify is a slogan.
- **Versioned** — nothing is deleted; superseded things are retained for the
  audit trail. The trail is the point.

## The horizons spine

Everything connects vertically, from today's actions to who you want to become:

```
Purpose & Principles   ← who you are / want to be
   Vision (3–5 yr)
      Goals (6–24 mo)
         Areas (ongoing roles & standards)
            Projects (finite outcomes)
               Actions (today)
```

A healthy life keeps these aligned. A project that serves no goal, a goal that
betrays a principle, an area you've abandoned for a month — these are the
failures the agent exists to catch. See [`coherence.md`](coherence.md).

## Three failure modes this guards against

1. **Borrowed** — rules inherited from books, mentors, or culture with no lived
   evidence. Tagged `aspirational` until your behavior earns them.
2. **Performed** — what you'd tell an interviewer you believe, not what you do
   under pressure. The `revealed` vs `aspirational` split makes the gap visible.
3. **Frozen** — a one-time reflection that never updates as life changes. The
   cadence (daily → annual) forces periodic falsification.

## Encompassing, not bloated

A life OS that tracks everything forever becomes a graveyard of dead goals and
fictional habits — another abandoned template. The antidote is the **pruning
loop**: every artifact, in every module, that earns no attention or evidence
across a full cycle becomes a candidate for `contested` and then retirement. The
review cadence is what keeps the system alive. Breadth without pruning is rot.
