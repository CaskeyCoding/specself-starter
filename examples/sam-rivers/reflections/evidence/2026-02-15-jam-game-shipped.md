# Shipped the jam build with placeholder art

## What happened

A 48-hour game jam ended Sunday at 6pm. By Saturday night I had a working core loop
and an ugly, programmer-art menu. The old me would have skipped sleep to make the
menu pretty and submitted at the deadline with no time to spare. Instead I posted the
rough build Saturday night and spent Sunday watching two strangers play it on a
stream. They both ignored the mechanic I was proudest of and latched onto a throwaway
one. I swapped which was central by Sunday afternoon and submitted a better game than
the "polished" version would have been.

## What it reveals

A hypothesis, not a conclusion: that for me, *real players earlier* beats *more
polish later* — and that my instinct to perfect before exposing is usually fear
wearing the costume of craft. This is the instance the `ship-before-its-ready`
principle is built on.
