# Aspirational: Who I Want to Become

## fund-health-like-a-load-bearing-area

- domain: health
- added: 2026-03-01

**The rule.** When a deadline presses, protect the two weekly runs and 7+ hours of
sleep the same way I protect a store-page date, not as the slack I spend first.

**Why it appeals.** It's partly borrowed: the version of this I actually envy is
other solo devs who post "shipped and rested" instead of "shipped and wrecked," and I
want that story more than I've earned it. The 2026-W09 weekly review found zero
health evidence against two late nights and both runs skipped that same week. The
honest read is that I fund this domain last, not first, and I want to be the kind of
person who doesn't.

**Promotion bar.** Four consecutive weeks where `area:health` evidence shows the
standard held (7+ hours most nights, both weekly runs kept), including at least one
week that overlaps a real deadline the way 2026-W09 did. Graduates to
`principles/health/` only after that pattern, not after one good week.
