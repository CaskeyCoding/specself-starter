# Ship a playable demo and a live wishlist page

A finite outcome with a finish line — which is what makes it a project and not an
area.

## Definition of done

- [ ] Store page is live with a wishlist button, screenshots, and a one-line hook.
- [ ] A 10-minute playable demo is downloadable from that page.
- [ ] The demo's first 60 seconds have been watched by 3 strangers and revised once
      from what they did (not what they said).

## Why it serves goal:launch-paid-game-v1

The goal needs 500 wishlists before launch; you can't gather wishlists without a page
and a demo. This project is the front of that funnel. It also lives out
`principle:ship-before-its-ready` — a *demo*, deliberately rough, exposed to real
players early.
