# Vision — Who I'm Becoming

## The portrait

I make small, finished games that a modest audience pays for, and I make them in
public. Each year I ship two or three, none of them perfect, all of them played. My
income is lumpy but real and mine — I answer to players, not a studio. My mornings
are quiet and I work rested; I stopped treating exhaustion as proof of seriousness.
People who follow my work know me as someone who *ships*, not someone with a great
unreleased game in a drawer.

## What has to be true

- I can take a game from idea to *paid* and shipped, repeatedly — not just to demo.
- I have a small audience that hears about a launch before it happens.
- I protect my health well enough to sustain the pace for years, not one sprint.

## What I'm willing to trade

I'll trade prestige and the approval of people who confuse polish with quality. I'll
trade the safety of a salary for the honesty of selling my own work. I will **not**
trade my health or my name — no dark-pattern monetization, no crunch as a lifestyle.

## What this is NOT

Not a studio with employees and a funding treadmill. Not a viral hit I chase by
copying trends. Not "respected by people I don't respect." The borrowed version of
this vision is "famous game dev"; the real one is "solvent, prolific, rested."
