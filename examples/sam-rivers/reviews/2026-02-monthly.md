in small, real steps (build compiling, store page draft, `decision:ADR-001` locking

## The deeper question (Therapist)

who Sam is becoming, but February's actual behavior treats a late night as the proof
the work mattered, and a run as the item that was optional. Worth sitting with, not
resolving here: is skipping the run really about the deadline, or is it that
finishing exhausted still feels like the only believable way to have earned the
finish?
